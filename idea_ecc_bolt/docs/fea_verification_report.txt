FEA vs experiment: 10/10 cases, mean 1.189 (measured / FE capacity)

Interpretation
--------------
The nonlinear fallback produces a capacity for every specimen and its mean
bias (1.188) is of the same order as the classical C&K bias (1.127).  The
per-specimen scatter is larger: the FE model is mesh-sensitive at the bearing
zone, the J2 update has an automatic elastic fallback for locally singular
bearing states, and S18/S21 are governed by the local plate criterion rather
than by bolt shear.  The FE result is therefore reported as a fallback and not
as a replacement for a commercial CBFEM calculation; the plate model actually
used is printed with every result.
